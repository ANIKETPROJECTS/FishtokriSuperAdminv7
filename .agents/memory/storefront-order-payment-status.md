---
name: Storefront payment lifecycle differs by source
description: Preserve FTN's legacy payment lifecycle and FTW pending, completed, and failed payment handling while showing completed as Paid in Admin.
---

## Rule
Treat FTN and FTW payment states separately. Keep FTN's existing paid-on-arrival behavior unchanged. FTW orders begin pending and remain on hold until payment completes; pending orders have a seven-minute payment window, but an explicit failed status is final and should move to Deleted immediately. Do not accept a later success callback after an order is marked failed. A completed payment displays as Paid across Admin payment-status views without changing its stored status. If an FTW update says unpaid while a positive total is fully covered by paidAmount and dueAmount is zero, normalize it to paid. In invoices, a paid status is authoritative over stale or missing paid/due amount fields: display the invoice total as paid and zero due. When a failed or timed-out FTW order moves to Deleted, restore inventory and refund only its wallet portion exactly once; retry failed refunds and do not backfill already-deleted orders unless explicitly requested. Payment auto-fixes must not overwrite pending or failed FTW records.

**Why:** The product rule is that a payment marked failed remains failed; only orders that stay pending use the seven-minute wait. Once a failed order is deleted, any wallet amount deducted at checkout must be returned without duplicate credits.

**How to apply:** When changing FT* payment callbacks, migrations, Admin payment logic, or invoice display, branch by order source instead of applying one payment lifecycle rule to all storefront orders. Accept successful FTW settlement only from pending; process a failed signal immediately through the atomic soft-delete and wallet-refund path. Keep the seven-minute expiry only for orders that remain pending. Normalize `completed` to Paid at the display/filter layer; normalize `unpaid` to `paid` only when the recorded paid amount covers the positive total and due is zero. When status is paid, derive invoice paid/due amounts from the displayed total rather than stale amount fields. Scope wallet refunds to that Mongo order and persist retry/idempotency state so a crash cannot cause duplicate credits.
