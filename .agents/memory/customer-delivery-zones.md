name: Customer delivery zones
description: Keep manually assigned delivery zones independent from pincode service-area behavior.
---

Zones are independent from pincodes: do not infer, calculate, or change zone assignments from pincode values. Configure zones per sub-hub and assign them manually to individual customer addresses.

**Why:** the user explicitly stated that zones have nothing to do with pincodes.

**How to apply:** future customer address and service-area work should preserve the separate zone list and its manual address assignment.
